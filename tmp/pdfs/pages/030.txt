GraphRAG combines retrieval-augmented generation with graph-aware context selection. By representing 
entities and their relationships in a graph store, the system can traverse multiple hops to assemble a holistic set 
of relevant facts. This improves completeness and reduces the chance that the model misses indirect 
relationships that are essential for accurate investment guidance.

traversals can be executed efficiently compared to chaining multiple vector searches and joining results in an 
application layer. The managed nature of Knowledge Bases and Neptune Analytics reduces maintenance, 
scaling, and operational burden while enabling strong performance.
